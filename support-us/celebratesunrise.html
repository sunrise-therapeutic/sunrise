Celebrate Sunrise!

"Celebrate Sunrise!" is our annual cornerstone fundraising event for our year-round therapy programs, traditionally held each spring.

We held this year's event on Sunday, June 14, 2026, when we again welcomed supporters, sponsors, volunteers, and community partners to celebrate our mission and the incredible impact of our programs.

As our signature fundraising celebration, Celebrate Sunrise! raises vital funds to support our year-round therapeutic riding programs for children and adults with disabilities. Thanks to the incredible generosity of our community, the 2026 event raised an incredible $107,000.

Guests enjoyed an unforgettable afternoon featuring:

  • Delicious food prepared by Little Mushroom Catering & Decadently Yours
  • An exciting live auction led by outstanding auctioneer Robert Milburn
  • A silent auction with a wide selection of great prizes
  • Opportunities to purchase gifts created by our talented Lifeskills group
  • The chance to learn more about Sunrise programs and meet some of our amazing horses

Sunrise is extremely grateful for all the support we received at our annual Celebrate Sunrise event, and we look forward to seeing you all again next year at our 2027 event!
