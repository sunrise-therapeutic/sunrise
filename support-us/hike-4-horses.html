Hike 4 Horses 2026

Calling all runners, hikers, dog walkers, horse lovers & families...
Join Sunrise Therapeutic Riding & Learning Centre at the farm for our second annual "Hike 4 Horses" event—a family-friendly fun afternoon where you can learn more about us and our amazing horses all while helping our charity raise funds.

Date: Sunday, September 27, 2026

Time: 1pm-4pm (Meet the horses and registration from 12:00-1:00 PM)

Location: 6920 Concession Road 1, Puslinch, ON N0B 2J0

What to expect:

  • Run or walk a 5k trail through the meadows and forests on our beautiful property
  • Lunch provided
  • Free Family Activities including Fire Trucks, meeting the fire department's Pluggie the robot fire hydrant & Sparky the mascot, face painting, loot and much more!
  • This year participants are welcome to bring their well-behaved dogs for the hike
  • All proceeds from this event support the care and well-being of our horses!
  • Collect pledges from family and friends online and/or make a donation yourself!  (All donations over $20 will receive a charitable tax receipt- Registered Charity # 11920 6027 RR0001)
